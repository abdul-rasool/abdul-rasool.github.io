Abdur Rasool, PhD

About Me

Abdur Rasool is a Postdoctoral Associate at the University of Hawaii, U.S.A. He received a PhD in computer applied technology (in jan. 2024) from Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (SIAT-CAS), Shenzhen, China. With research experience at:

  • University of Hawaii, USA (1 year)
    under the supervision of Peter Yiğitcan Washington and John A. Shepherd, PhD; and in collaboration with Dennis Wall.
  • SIAT-CAS, Shenzhen (3.5 years)
    under the supervision of Prof. Qingshan Jiang and Qiang Qu; and in collaboration with Junbiao Dai.
  • Donghua University, Shanghai (1.5 years)
    (Supervisor information not provided)

He has authored over 40 publications in international journals and academic conferences in the data sciences domain, particularly in bioinformatics, DNA data storage, NLP/LLMs, and information security. He was an excellent international student and an outstanding graduate of the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) and a winner of various research contests. His interests are in multidisciplinary computer science and technology, mainly in AI, Bioinformatics, DNA data storage, and LLMs.
